## Top confirmed correctness defects (cross-dimension, deduped)

1. **Alpha is positive-by-construction** — the t-stat≥1.0 window censor makes reported alpha a
   conditional median over significant-only windows; min alpha across all 665 funds is **+1.13%**,
   no fund can show negative alpha (`compute/alpha.py:30,76`). Found independently by 4 auditors.
   At 0.25 (stage 1) / 0.20 (stage 2) weight, the heaviest-weighted score input cannot represent
   underperformance.
2. **Null metrics score as category-average** — `_weighted_sum` zeroes NaN z-scores
   (`rank/score.py:70-74`); the not-null alpha gate promised in `alpha.py`'s docstring does not
   exist. 54 of 435 stage-1 survivors have null alpha. Confirmed consequence: HDFC Defence ranked
   #1 of 37 in Thematic with exactly ONE non-null metric.
3. **Universe-exclusion bug** — scheme_master's option parser recognizes only "growth"/"idcw", so
   ICICI Pru growth plans named "Cumulative" (4 large funds incl. Equity Savings, 11.5y history)
   never enter the universe at all (`master/scheme_master.py:129-133`).
4. **Zero-NAV poisoning** — 211,859 zero NAVs flow into log-return computation; NaN metrics are
   stored with `data_quality_flag=GOOD`; NaN funds pass some filters because polars treats NaN>x
   as true (`compute/alignment.py:92`).
5. **Dead/stale funds ranked fresh** — compute anchors each scheme to its own last NAV date with no
   as_of staleness check; 5 ranked funds have last NAV from 2025 or earlier
   (`compute/orchestrator.py:46`).
6. **Stage 3 wipes 5 of 26 categories from the final report** — ELSS, Flexi Cap, Balanced Advantage,
   Equity Savings, Contra get zero final picks; the greedy cross-category drop has no per-category
   floor and never reconsiders (Parag Parikh Flexi Cap dropped in favor of a fund itself dropped 71
   steps later) (`rank/stage3.py:101-143`).
7. **Stage 2 punishes missing data harder than bad data** — missing PTR is fatal (11/14 stage-2
   drops, incl. Multi Cap stage-1 #1 and #2 and Thematic #1) while terrible-but-present PTR costs
   at most 0.05 (`rank/stage2.py:45-49`).
8. **Dead config everywhere** — pipeline.yaml filter thresholds, beta_bands,
   `min_history_years_for_ranking: 5`, `top_n_per_category: 10` are loaded but ignored by hardcoded
   values; active_share's 0.10 stage-2 weight has never activated (constituents exist for one
   month); stage-2 weights sum to 0.85, not the commented 0.95.
9. **Kotak holdings retry pathology** — ~2.6h per run (88% of the last 3h pipeline run) retrying
   downloads for an unpublished month (`ingest/holdings/kotak.py:153`).
10. **Holdings cross-contamination instance** — Motilal Multi Factor FoF's portfolio stored under
    Motilal Multi Cap (subset names score 100 in token_set fuzzy match; the systemic mechanism is
    `ingest/managers/_scheme_match.py:206`).

## Structural conclusions

- **Value-add (dim 3):** the pipeline's entire value proposition is "picks beat the category-median
  fund by ~+4.1pp 3y / +2.7pp 5y *if trailing rank persists*" — and nothing in the repo tests
  persistence (zero backtest code). SPIVA India says large-cap active mostly loses to the index;
  in the live DB, in 8+ categories (Value, MNC, PSU, Manufacturing, Auto, FMCG, Mid Cap, Large Cap)
  the median fund trails its TRI benchmark, yet the pipeline still emits top-5 picks and can never
  recommend the index instead.
- **Survivorship (dim 6):** scheme_master is rebuilt from today's AMFI listing with
  `is_active=True` hardcoded; 12,874 dead scheme codes with NAV history are invisible. All beat
  rates and category z-stats are survivor-only.
- **Retail completeness (dim 4):** "sufficient-only-with-X": no exit/switching cost model
  (LTCG/STCG/exit loads have zero hits in the repo), no monitoring/deterioration story, no
  drawdown framing, no debt/gold/international coverage, raw 42-column CSVs as the interface.
- **Speed (dim 1c):** collection is ~98% of wall-clock; first full run ~13-16h (T-bill walk
  ~10.5h); monthly refresh should be ~45-75min but is ~3h due to the kotak pathology; everything
  per-AMC is serial. Compute is a non-issue (~85s for 665 funds).
- **The systemic pattern:** breakage is mostly loud (good per-AMC isolation, transactional writes,
  parse-skip caches), but **wrongness is mostly silent** — fuzzy-match poaching, wrong-month
  ingestion, NaN-through-GOOD, null-as-average, dead config, dormant adapters.

### 1b. Collection robustness (parsing & scraping brittleness) — grade B-

The ingestion layer is unusually defensive at the AMC granularity — zero-row parses raise IngestError (AMFI NAV, AAUM, benchmarks, holdings discovery), per-AMC fault isolation contains 4xx and parse crashes, DELETE+upsert runs in one transaction, and the parse-skip cache requires artifact-hash + match-universe + DB-rows agreement. A full upstream format change therefore fails loud. The danger lives one level down, at the per-scheme granularity, where almost everything fails silent. Confirmed live wrongness: quant's WebForms endpoint served April portfolio files for a May request; 29 schemes' April holdings (banner "AS ON 30 Apr 2026", byte-identical to the April files) sit in holdings_monthly stamped 2026-05-01, and the poisoned month-keyed cache means real May data can never be ingested without manual deletion. No adapter validates the artifact's internal statement date. Scheme-name fuzzy matching is the second systemic silent-wrongness mechanism: token_set_ratio scores subset names at 100, so when the true scheme is absent from scheme_master a sibling silently receives its PTR/holdings — a failure mode that has already occurred repeatedly (kotak/dsp/quant adapters carry reactive per-name fixups), and the managers PTR path has no best-score collision resolution at all (first parse-order record wins). Factsheet PDF extraction is geometry-calibrated against one month's PDF per AMC; a redesign usually yields silent missing PTR (guarded no-op), but the word-position fallbacks can pick an adjacent number silently, with only a >0 sanity check. Holdings re-runs DELETE the whole (amc, month) partition and reinsert only this run's successes, so partial per-scheme failures or shrunken discovery silently delete data. HTTP fetch does no content validation, so a WAF HTML shell served as 200 (proven real by trustmf.com) permanently poisons the per-month cache. AMFI NAV parsing itself fails loud on format change but accepts arbitrarily partial volumes, and `mfs pipeline` only snapshots today's NAVAll — the verified 2026-05-23..06-04 hole and the 823-row 2026-06-08 day were accepted by all gates. TER is a manual-CSV stub. Net: breakage is mostly loud; wrongness is mostly silent.

### 6. Methodology biases (survivorship, look-ahead, overfitting) [auditor-added] — grade C-

Survivorship: yes, structurally. scheme_master is rebuilt from today's AMFI NAVAll with is_active hardcoded True (scheme_master.py:206) and written via TRUNCATE+COPY (writers.py:146), so any fund that closed or merged simply vanishes. Empirically 12,874 scheme codes in nav_daily are absent from scheme_master, with last-NAV dates spread 2014-2026 — dead funds exist in the data but are invisible to ranking, and category z-scores compare survivors only. Point-in-time integrity is inconsistent: AUM is bounded by as_of with an explicit look-ahead comment (orchestrator.py:241), but PTR and holdings in the same function take the unbounded latest month, and shortlist's AUM map ignores as_of entirely; active share is correctly month-aligned but null universe-wide; quant PTR stamped 2024-03 feeds 2026 rankings (documented). Benchmarks are uniformly TRI (no PRI dividend bias — a genuine strength), but assignment is category-level, not per-scheme SEBI mandate, and fit is demonstrably poor in several categories (Value: median R² 0.727, beta 0.58; Energy: R² 0.580, median "alpha" 13.8%/yr). Selection-on-noise: six categories have 1-4 funds post-filter (FMCG ranks a single fund with composite 0.0); Stage 2 re-z-scores within ≤20 then ≤5-fund cohorts. Overfitting/validation: filters.py's own docstring admits thresholds were relaxed in-sample to rescue a favored fund; weights have no out-of-sample support; and a point-in-time backtest is impossible because the schema keeps no history of universe, category, benchmark, or constituents. Finally, Stage 2's required-metric rule dropped Multi Cap's stage-1 #1 and #2 for missing PTR — disclosure coverage, not merit, decided the category. Solid engineering, but the rankings carry a systematic optimistic tilt and cannot currently be validated.

### 8. Operational robustness: crash-safety, idempotency, staleness gates [auditor-added] — grade B

Crash-safety is genuinely strong: truncate-reloads (risk_free_daily, scheme_master) run TRUNCATE+COPY in one transaction (writers.py:124-130, 144-151) so a kill cannot leave them empty; holdings/PTR and computed_metrics partitions use DELETE+reinsert in a single transaction (holdings/_run.py:176-182, managers/_run.py:170-177, writers.py:213-217); parse-skip markers are written only post-commit via atomic writes; the advisory lock auto-releases on process death. The one crash window is NAV-commit-then-log-returns-refresh in two transactions with no divergence detector, and compute reads that cache. Idempotency is also good: every writer is upsert-or-replace, and the old skip-on-presence regression was replaced by content-hash parse-skip (_parse_cache.py) — though presence-trust survives at the fetch layer (cached PDFs/Excels never revalidated, even under --full).

Staleness is where the flagship invariant breaks. If AMFI is down today, the pipeline halts cleanly (ingest_today wraps failures in IngestError, exit 2). But the pipeline's NAV stage is `ingest_today` only — a single latest-NAV snapshot — so days between runs are never backfilled, and neither freshness.py nor the Gate A contract examines interior calendar gaps (only end-recency and per-entity latest). Proven live: nav_daily has ~8 missing trading days (2026-05-25..06-04), Gate A passed 655/665, and the 2026-06-08 run ranked 665 funds over that hole, directly contradicting run-pipeline.md's "the coverage gate proves the gap actually closed." If holdings are 3 months stale, the pipeline ranks anyway — Phase-2 freshness lags are all null and Gate B never halts (documented, deliberate). Failure handling: per-AMC isolation with an all-fail backstop matches the sanctioned "skip the AMC" rule, but uncaught 4xx still crashes required stages (bhavcopy, scheme_master, amfi_aum) as raw tracebacks — a known open item.

Recovery: re-run `mfs pipeline` from zero; no resume machinery needed since all writes are idempotent/atomic. Worst case is tens of minutes (holdings re-parses all ~1,357 Excels every run — no parse-skip). The real footgun: missed NAV days require a manual `mfs ingest navs --since` that nothing prompts for.
